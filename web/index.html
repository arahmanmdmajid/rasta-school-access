Data and method

What the numbers mean
Distance is to the nearest mapped school, estimated in a straight line × 1.3 detour factor ÷ 4 km/h — about 769 m for 15 minutes. Not routed along roads. The 15-minute threshold comes from the UNESCO Global Education Monitoring Report 2026.
How complete is the map
Only about 13% of Sindh's schools appear in open data: 6,390 mapped against roughly 48,000 on the official count. So every output is a list of places to field-verify, never a confirmed gap. A district showing few schools is telling you about the map, not about the district.
Child counts are estimates
A national age share is applied to gridded population, so figures are modelled estimates and not census counts.
